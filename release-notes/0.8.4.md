# TPS Calendar Base 0.8.4

Very short timed records now remain large enough to show their title in full Calendar Base views as well as embeds.

- Every dedicated, mobile, embedded, and continuous time grid gives timed event geometry an 18px visual floor.
- The event's top remains anchored to its exact authored start. When the real interval renders shorter than 18px, FullCalendar extends only the visual bottom; the stored end and duration are not changed.
- FullCalendar's own geometry performs the extension, so collision layout, hit testing, selection, drag, and resize use the same visible footprint rather than allowing a CSS-only card to cover a neighbor.
- Naturally taller records retain their proportional height. All-day rows and month views are unchanged.
- Dedicated Calendar views retain their configured zoom and time-row density. Embedded views retain their existing 18px condensed-row safeguard.

This is a backward-compatible rendering correction with no settings, Base-definition, record-data, command, or persistence migration. Minimum supported Obsidian remains 1.10.0.
