# TPS Calendar Base 0.3.13

## Intent

Correct the displayed days in ordinary multi-day calendars by restoring the selected date as the focal day without changing exact filter-derived ranges.

## User-visible fixes

- Manual `2d` through `7d` views center the saved, selected, Today, or date-picker day using the established left-biased center for even day counts. A three-day view on Friday now renders Thursday through Saturday.
- Previous and Next shift the focal day without accumulating an offset; reloads, FullCalendar remounts, and responsive column-count changes preserve that same focal day.
- Responsive partial-week layouts keep the focal date in view, while complete seven-day weeks remain aligned to the configured first weekday.
- Automatic filter-based ranges with explicit date bounds remain start-anchored and preserve their exact inclusive span.
- Manual views that also contain scheduled-date filters no longer inherit automatic navigation bounds that can clip the centered leading columns.

## Compatibility and migration

- Minimum supported Obsidian version remains 1.10.0.
- No settings, Base schema, commands, public API, or note data change.
- Existing `tps_currentDate` values are preserved; only their intended focal-day interpretation is restored for manual multi-day views.

## Validation

- All 132 release-declared tests passed. Focused date-range coverage validates manual `2d` through `7d`, responsive partial weeks, exact explicit ranges, Monday/Sunday full weeks, Today/date-picker/navigation/reload round trips, and spring/fall daylight-saving boundaries.
- TypeScript and the mandatory separate production-mode build passed. The final source artifacts match the deployed test runtime byte-for-byte; only `main.js` and `manifest.json` changed, while the complete BRAT stylesheet bundles remained byte-identical.
- After a forced reload in Obsidian 1.12.7, the saved July 31 manual three-day fixture rendered July 30–August 1. Next rendered July 31–August 2 and Previous restored July 30–August 1 without changing the saved focal date.
- The exact two-day fixture remained July 31–August 1, and the exact six-day fixture remained July 31–August 5. The original three-day QA view and its `tps_currentDate: 2026-07-31` state were restored after inspection.
- No notes, settings, feeds, or outbound automation changed. Production was not accessed; the public release is the BRAT handoff.

## Known limitations

- Entry-derived filter-based views preserve the user's navigated focal date when matching records change; only explicitly bounded automatic ranges snap to their derived lower bound.
