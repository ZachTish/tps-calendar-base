# TPS Calendar Base 0.3.9

## Intent

Keep filter-derived calendar days exact on every surface, including narrow Markdown, Canvas, and mobile embeds.

## User-visible fix

- Filter-based one-through-seven-day ranges retain their full derived column count in constrained embeds.
- A six-day July 31–August 5 range no longer reuses its centered August 2 anchor after responsive reduction and therefore cannot begin on the wrong midpoint day.
- Manual `3d`, `7d`, and week embeds keep their established width-responsive column reduction. This patch does not change ordinary manual-view navigation.

## Compatibility and migration

- Minimum supported Obsidian version remains 1.10.0.
- No setting, Base schema, command, plugin API, or note data changes.
- Existing filter-based and manual view configurations remain valid.

## Validation

- Focused regression coverage composes exact filter bounds, centered semantic anchors, constrained embed width, and final rendered start dates for every two-through-seven-day range.
- All 131 tests passed, including three contained-workspace checks, followed by TypeScript and the required production-mode build.
- The final 0.3.9 artifacts deployed to `Obsidian Plugin Test Vault`, and `Reload app without saving` reloaded Obsidian 1.12.7.
- A constrained Markdown embed of `_archive/Calendar Exact Days QA.base#Exact 6 days` rendered Fri Jul 31 through Wed Aug 5 as six columns. The same 0.3.8 path could reduce to one or two columns beginning at Sun Aug 2.
- The temporary visual-QA note was moved to `_archive`; no outbound automation was enabled and production was not accessed.

## Known limitations

- Exact six- or seven-day filter ranges intentionally remain dense in very narrow embeds. Use a shorter filtered range or a manual responsive view when fewer simultaneous columns are preferred.
