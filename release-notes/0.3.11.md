# TPS Calendar Base 0.3.11

## Intent

Restore the saved-date meaning that manual multi-day calendars used before 0.3.10, without regressing exact filter-derived ranges.

## User-visible fixes

- Manual two-through-seven-day views center the saved, selected, or Today date within the rendered range. A Friday selection in `3d` renders Thursday through Saturday.
- Responsive manual ranges recalculate around the same focal day when their visible column count changes.
- Exact filter-bounded ranges remain start-anchored at their lower bound, including dense six- and seven-day constrained embeds.
- Full weeks continue to align to the configured first weekday and navigate by seven local calendar days.
- Rendered-start-to-selected-day conversion is now the inverse of selected-day-to-rendered-start conversion, so `tps_currentDate` survives reloads and navigation without drifting.

## Compatibility and migration

- Minimum supported Obsidian version remains 1.10.0.
- No settings, Base-schema, command, plugin-API, or note-data migration is required.
- Manual multi-day views saved while using 0.3.10 will again interpret `tps_currentDate` as the focal day, matching 0.3.9 and earlier behavior.

## Validation

- Focused tests cover centered manual windows, start-anchored exact ranges, all two-through-seven-day round trips, responsive day counts, week alignment, bounded navigation, and daylight-saving transitions.
- The complete declared suite, TypeScript, the required separate production-mode build, and reloaded test-vault UI verification are required before publication.

## Known limitations

- Even-numbered manual ranges place the focal day in the earlier of the two center positions, matching the historical behavior.

## Release artifacts

SHA-256 hashes are recorded after the final tested build.
