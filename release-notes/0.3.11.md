# TPS Calendar Base 0.3.11

## Intent

Restore the saved-date meaning that manual multi-day calendars used before 0.3.10, without regressing exact filter-derived ranges.

## User-visible fixes

- Manual two-through-seven-day views center the saved, selected, or Today date within the rendered range. A Friday selection in `3d` renders Thursday through Saturday.
- Responsive manual ranges recalculate around the same focal day when their visible column count changes.
- Automatic filter-based ranges with explicit date bounds remain start-anchored at their lower bound, including dense six- and seven-day constrained embeds. Manually configured views stay centered even when their Base has date filters.
- Full weeks continue to align to the configured first weekday and navigate by seven local calendar days.
- Rendered-start-to-selected-day conversion is now the inverse of selected-day-to-rendered-start conversion, so `tps_currentDate` survives reloads and navigation without drifting.

## Compatibility and migration

- Minimum supported Obsidian version remains 1.10.0.
- No settings, Base-schema, command, plugin-API, or note-data migration is required.
- Manual multi-day views saved while using 0.3.10 will again interpret `tps_currentDate` as the focal day, matching 0.3.9 and earlier behavior.

## Validation

- All 131 declared tests passed from a clean release worktree, including centered manual windows, start-anchored exact ranges, all two-through-seven-day round trips, responsive day counts, week alignment, bounded navigation, and spring/fall daylight-saving transitions.
- Five shared workspace-containment checks, TypeScript, and the required separate production-mode build passed. The build atomically deployed only byte-changed runtime artifacts to the test vault.
- After `Reload app without saving` in Obsidian 1.12.7, a saved Jul 31 manual three-day view rendered Jul 30–Aug 1 and retained Jul 31. An automatic explicit range still rendered exactly Jul 31–Aug 5. A temporary manually pinned three-day view with those same date filters also rendered Jul 30–Aug 1 and retained Jul 31 after persistence.
- The temporary QA view was removed, fixture state was restored, no outbound automation was enabled, and production was not accessed.

## Known limitations

- Even-numbered manual ranges place the focal day in the earlier of the two center positions, matching the historical behavior.
